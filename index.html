Say it.

Type or talk, mid-walk or at midnight. Half-formed is fine. Lumen replies and works out what you meant.

Every open loop lands in Lumen and stays there: the call you owe, the idea you can't drop. It remembers, so you don't have to.

Do it.

Say "make it a goal" and the steps appear. Habits, tasks and alarms on your phone's clock work the same way.
