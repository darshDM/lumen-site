Privacy policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Lumen is an app where you talk about your day, your goals and how you feel, and Lumen measures patterns in what you say. That means you trust us with personal things. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who processes it, and how you delete it.

Questions: darshmamtora2122@gmail.com

What we collect

DataWhy
Google account name, email address and Google account ID (from Google Sign-In) To create and identify your account. We never see your Google password.
What you write or say in Lumen: conversations, your onboarding intro, goals, tasks, habits, alarms and mood check-ins This is the app: Lumen replies to you and counts patterns in it.
Voice recordings Turned into text. We do not store audio; only the transcript is kept.
Measurements derived from your messages (for example mood, topic, the goal a message is about) and a short written summary of you that Lumen keeps updated To show your Insights and to give replies that remember you.
Your time zone and reminder settings To send habit reminders, follow-ups and check-ins at the right local time.
Safety screening records (that a message was flagged and what Lumen did; not the message itself) If a message suggests you may be in crisis, Lumen shows support resources. We keep a record of that event.
Technical server logs To fix errors and keep the service running. Kept for up to 30 days.

Some data stays only on your phone: the profile details in Settings, your sign-in token (stored encrypted), and the reminders scheduled on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. Lumen has no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Who processes your data

We use a small number of service providers that process data only to run Lumen for you:

  • OpenAI: your messages are sent to OpenAI's API to write Lumen's replies, measure your messages and find related past entries. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models, and it is deleted within 30 days.
  • Groq: voice recordings are sent to Groq to turn them into text. Groq does not use API data to train models and keeps it only briefly.
  • Google: sign-in only.
  • Oracle Cloud: hosts our server and database.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

How we protect it

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Every request is authenticated and can only read your own data. Access to the server is limited to the developer.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists, because Lumen's value comes from patterns over months. You can delete a single conversation at any time in the app; that removes it and everything measured from it. When you delete your account, all your data is deleted (see below).

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete account. Or email us from your account's email address. Your account and all data in our database are deleted, and any remaining copies in logs are gone within 30 days. Full details: Delete your account.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing darshmamtora2122@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, California or India), you may have more rights under local law; we honor them.

Not medical advice

Lumen is a self-reflection tool, not a medical or mental health service, and it does not diagnose or treat anything. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services or a helpline.

Age

Lumen is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
