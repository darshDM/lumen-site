LumenPrivacy policy

Delete your Lumen account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Lumen and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email darshmamtora2122@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Lumen account", from the Google email address you signed in with. We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: name, email and Google account ID
  • All conversations, messages and transcripts
  • Everything measured from them, and Lumen's summary of you
  • Goals, tasks, habits and check-ins, alarms, follow-ups and mood check-ins
  • Settings, time zone and safety screening records

What is kept

Nothing tied to you. Server logs that may reference your account are erased within 30 days. Our AI providers (OpenAI, Groq) delete the data they processed within 30 days under their own policies; they never use it for training.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.
